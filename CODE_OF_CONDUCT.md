# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Alumni Portugal is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity,
background, ability, appearance, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behaviour

Community members should:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and respect;
- give constructive feedback focused on ideas and work;
- respect privacy, consent, and personal boundaries;
- acknowledge mistakes and make a good-faith effort to repair harm;
- help keep technical and community spaces useful and inclusive.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- deliberate intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation, spam, or other conduct intended to deceive or harm;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in organization repositories, issues, pull requests,
Discussions, events, and other spaces where someone represents Alumni Portugal.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to an Alumni Portugal organization owner or
another trusted community administrator. Do not disclose sensitive details in
a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably
possible, and conflicts of interest will be avoided when assigning reviewers.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or
permanently remove participants whose behaviour violates this policy. Actions
will be proportionate to the behaviour, its impact, and any pattern of prior
conduct.

Good-faith reports and appeals will be reviewed without retaliation.
